Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Your privacy matters. The Pulsation Apple Watch app does not collect any personal data or share any data with third parties.

1. Data Collection

Pulsation does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app does not require you to create an account or provide any information that could be used to identify you.

2. Health and Fitness Data

Pulsation does not collect or transmit health or fitness data. No data from Apple Watch sensors or Apple Health is sent to any server or third party.

3. Third-Party Services

Pulsation does not include advertising services, analytics tools, or third-party services designed to collect user data.

4. Data Security and Retention

Since Pulsation does not collect personal data, it does not retain any personal data on a server.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or privacy practices change. The last updated date will be revised accordingly.
